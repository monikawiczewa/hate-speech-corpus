- Ten cleaned files concatenated: **257,590 rows**, including ten literal `intent` labels in `value_counts()` and at least one `sentence,intent` header row. These are notebook-recorded outputs, not independently recomputed file counts.

- The notebook **oversamples before** `train_test_split` (first 60/40, then half of remaining), with `random_state=42`. Saved output shapes: **409,314 train; 136,438 validation; 136,438 test**.
- Combined cleaned and tokenized files are saved using `mode='a'` (append), making repeated notebook runs non-idempotent.
- Paths refer to the author's original Google Colab Drive directory.

## Critical methodological concern: evaluation leakage

**Oversampling before splitting can place copies of the same original sentence into both training and evaluation sets.** Therefore these supplied splits should **not** be presented as independent holdout sets without a full overlap audit. The current release is historical research material, not a validated benchmark. A corrected experimental workflow should split by original records (and ideally by duplicate groups and upstream sources) **before** applying oversampling to training only.

## Corpus totals are stage-specific

The dissertation reports **2,187,310 sentences before preprocessing** and **12 original subjective categories** (Chapter 4, p.20). The notebook records **257,590 concatenated cleaned rows**, with ten substantive categories plus erroneous header records. The balanced dataset contains **682,190 records**, many generated by duplication through random oversampling. These numbers must not be described interchangeably or as unique sentences.

## Provenance and licensing

The dissertation's Appendix A captions conflict in places with its Appendix D and file names. In particular, Dataset 3 has a Jigsaw toxicity category despite a Waseem and Hovy attribution; Datasets 1 and 7 have near-identical class distributions. Source-specific licences and platform permissions have **not** been independently verified. No blanket licence or new DOI is recommended for the combined text at this stage.
