# Bibliography and attribution notes

This bibliography supports [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The ten numbered collections are not necessarily ten independent original publications. Some historical appendix attributions are unresolved.

- Davidson, T., Warmsley, D., Macy, M., and Weber, I. (2017). *Automated Hate Speech Detection and the Problem of Offensive Language*. ICWSM. [Original repository](https://github.com/t-davidson/hate-speech-and-offensive-language). Relevant to Datasets 1 and 7; exact duplication requires verification.
- Founta, A.-M., et al. (2018). *Large Scale Crowdsourcing and Characterization of Twitter Abusive Behavior*. ICWSM. Relevant to Dataset 2.
- de Gibert, O., Perez, N., García-Pablos, A., and Cuadros, M. (2018). *Hate Speech Dataset from a White Supremacy Forum*. Proceedings of ALW2, 11–20. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/W18-5102). **Confirmed for Dataset 5 by its bundled README and CC BY-SA 3.0 Spain licence**; the dissertation appendix's Figea attribution conflicts.
- Waseem, Z., and Hovy, D. (2016). *Hateful Symbols or Hateful People? Predictive Features for Hate Speech Detection on Twitter*. NAACL Student Research Workshop. [Source repository](https://github.com/zeeraktalat/hatespeech). Relevant to Dataset 6 and possibly other components.
- Jha, A., and Mamidi, R. (2017). *When Does a Compliment Become Sexist? Analysis and Classification of Ambivalent Sexism Using Twitter Data*. Workshop on NLP and Computational Social Science. [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/W17-2902/). Relevant to Dataset 8.
- Zampieri, M., Malmasi, S., Nakov, P., Rosenthal, S., Farra, N., and Kumar, R. (2019). *Predicting the Type and Target of Offensive Posts in Social Media*. NAACL-HLT. [OLID distribution](https://sites.google.com/site/offensevalsharedtask/olid). Relevant to Dataset 10.
- Jigsaw / Conversation AI toxicity corpus. A component is reported for Dataset 3, but the precise edition, formal citation and upstream-text terms require reconciliation.

## Unresolved historical references
Appendix A also names Fortuna and Nunes (2018), Lima et al. (2018), Mathew et al. (2020), Zannettou et al. (2018), and Figea et al. (2016). These are retained as **historical references**, not asserted as independently confirmed row-level origins. Dataset 9's original cyberbullying publication is not established.

## How to cite this research
Cite Wiczewa (2025), *Subjectivity Spaces in Natural Language Processing*, for the integrated historical MSc corpus and its original experiments. Cite upstream works for reused source datasets. Cite [AUDIT_FINDINGS.md](AUDIT_FINDINGS.md) and the GitHub commit for the 2026 corrected split. The corrected split has **no newly established model accuracy results**.
